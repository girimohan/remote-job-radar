    Each morning

    1. Open Jobs, tick “Only jobs first seen today”, and read the top ten.
    2. Expand a job to see why it matched and where it is open to. If it says “Check the posting”, open it and look for a location or work-authorisation line before spending time on it.
    3. Mark what you will not apply to as “Not for me” so it stays out of the way.

    Applying

    1. Press Open posting. Applications always go through the company's own form; this tool never applies for you.
    2. Press Copy tailoring prompt, paste it into Claude together with your CV, and use the result to adjust your CV summary and write the short answers.
    3. Where the form asks for location or work authorisation, say: based in Finland (EU), available as a contractor invoicing through your own registered business, or as an employee through an employer of record.
    4. Set the status to “Applied” and note the date and any contact name.

    What the labels mean

    • Europe, Finland, Worldwide: the posting says so in its location or description.
    • Check the posting: it is remote but does not say from where. Many of these turn out to be US-only.
    • One European country: remote, but tied to a country such as the UK or Germany. Usually needs residence there; worth asking only when the fit is very strong.

    The AI-checked tag

    Rules judge each job from its location field and a few phrases. Then an AI model (Claude) reads the whole posting and says where it can be done from, whether contracts are possible and which languages it needs. It has to quote the sentence it relied on; if that quote is not found word for word in the posting, its answer is thrown away and the rules decide. “AI-checked” means the answer passed that check.

    Finnish and other portals

    Duunitori, Oikotie and LinkedIn do not allow automated collection, and Työmarkkinatori needs a registered account for its interface. For those, the Jobs tab has a “Search other portals” list: pick a role on the left and each link opens that portal already searching for it.

    Postings written in Finnish or German are tagged and scored lower, because they usually need that language.

    Change what you see

    Roles, keywords, skills and the score cut-off live in config.yaml in the repository. Edit it there and the next daily run uses it.

    Where the jobs come from

    Company career pages on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters, and listings from Himalayas, Remote OK, Remotive, We Work Remotely, Jobicy, Arbeitnow, Working Nomads and the Hacker News “Who is hiring?” thread. Every job links to its original posting.
